## 2. Best Buy — Supply Chain Risk

**Question:**  
What supply chain risks does Best Buy disclose?

**Answer:**  
Best Buy discloses the following supply chain risks:

- The ability to maintain and upgrade the technology infrastructure supporting retail and supply chain operations is critical and poses a risk [Source 3].
- There is a risk to employees, customers, and inventory from burglaries or robberies during transit, at stores, or at other facilities [Source 3].
- The reliance on third parties to meet standards or commitments can impact operations [Source 3].
- Growing supply chain capacity needs, such as fulfillment services and handling returns, present additional challenges [Source 3].
- The transportation and distribution sectors face consolidation, business failures, and heightened political scrutiny, which could adversely affect operations [Source 3].
